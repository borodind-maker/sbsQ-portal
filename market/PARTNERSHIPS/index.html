    Partnership Strategy

    SmartBees Quantum (sbsQ) - Ecosystem Development

    Document Type: Partnership Strategy
    Version: 1.0
    Last Updated: 2026-01-14
    Classification: Business Confidential

    G1. Partnership Philosophy

    Core Principle: We don't compete with integrators—we enable them.

    sbsQ is a platform play, not a vertical solution. Our success depends on building an ecosystem where: - Integrators add value on top of our technology - Customers get complete solutions (not just components) - Partners become force multipliers for market reach

    G2. Partner Categories

    Tier 1: Integration Partners (Primary Channel)

    Who They Are: - Security/perimeter system integrators - UAV fleet management providers - Industrial robotics solution providers - Critical infrastructure monitoring platforms

    What They Do: - Bundle sbsQ into complete solutions - Provide domain expertise + implementation - Own customer relationship - Deliver ongoing support

    Value Proposition for Partner: - Differentiation: Offer physics-verified autonomy (unique in market) - Risk reduction: Fewer incidents = happier customers - Compliance enabler: Audit-ready artifacts simplify certification - Recurring revenue: Annual licenses create predictable income

    Revenue Model: - Reseller margin: 20–30% on license fees - Services revenue: 100% of integration/support fees (partner keeps) - Rev-share (optional): 15% of annual renewals

    Tier 2: Technology Partners

    Who They Are: - Sensor manufacturers (IMU, GNSS, vision, LIDAR) - Edge compute platforms (NVIDIA, Qualcomm, Intel) - Simulation/digital twin providers - Autonomy stack vendors (ROS, PX4, ArduPilot)

    What They Do: - Co-develop integrations - Joint reference architectures - Co-marketing (whitepapers, webinars) - Mutual customer referrals

    Value Proposition for Partner: - Enhanced product value: "Our sensors + sbsQ = certified autonomy" - Market access: Cross-sell to each other's customer bases - Technical credibility: Joint validation/case studies

    Revenue Model: - No direct revenue share (strategic, not transactional) - Joint pilots: Share cost, share credit - Co-marketing budget: Mutual investment in content/events

    Tier 3: Channel Partners (Regional/Vertical)

    Who They Are: - Regional distributors (EMEA, APAC, LATAM) - Vertical-specific resellers (energy, defense, logistics) - System integrators with niche expertise

    What They Do: - Localize sales/support - Navigate regional regulations - Provide vertical domain knowledge

    Value Proposition for Partner: - Exclusive territories (optional, for high performers) - Local language support (we provide, they deliver) - Co-branded materials

    Revenue Model: - Reseller margin: 15–25% - Deal registration: Protected margins for registered opportunities - MDF (Market Development Funds): Up to 5% of annual sales for co-marketing

    G3. Partner Enablement Package

    What We Provide to Partners

    1. Technical Enablement

    • ✅ SDK + documentation
    • ✅ Reference integration (ROS, PX4, ArduPilot)
    • ✅ Demo scenario + replay tools
    • ✅ Integration training (online + on-site option)
    • ✅ Technical pre-sales support

    2. Sales Enablement

    • ✅ Partner portal (deal registration, collateral, training)
    • ✅ Sales playbook (objection handling, competitive positioning)
    • ✅ Demo kit (hardware + software, loaner program)
    • ✅ ROI calculator (customizable for customer pitches)
    • ✅ Case studies + testimonials

    3. Marketing Support

    • ✅ Co-branded collateral (datasheets, presentations)
    • ✅ Joint webinars (quarterly)
    • ✅ Press release templates
    • ✅ Social media assets
    • ✅ Event sponsorship (select conferences)

    4. Commercial Support

    • ✅ Deal registration system (protect margins)
    • ✅ Quote generation tool
    • ✅ Partner pricing (tiered discounts)
    • ✅ Quarterly business reviews

    G4. Partner Onboarding Process

    Phase 1: Qualification (Week 1)

    Activities: - Initial call: Assess fit (market, capabilities, commitment) - NDA execution - Technical assessment (can they integrate?) - Business assessment (do they have customer pipeline?)

    Decision: Proceed to onboarding or decline

    Phase 2: Onboarding (Weeks 2–4)

    Activities: - Partner agreement execution - Portal access provisioning - Technical training (2-day workshop) - Sales training (1-day workshop) - First demo setup

    Deliverables: - Certified partner status - Demo environment - Co-branded collateral

    Phase 3: First Deal (Weeks 5–12)

    Activities: - Joint opportunity identification - Co-selling (sbsQ + partner) - Pilot execution (if needed) - Deal closure

    Success Metric: First revenue within 90 days of onboarding

    Phase 4: Scale (Ongoing)

    Activities: - Quarterly business reviews - Pipeline development - Co-marketing campaigns - Feedback loop (product/process improvements)

    Success Metric: 3+ deals/year, $200K+ annual revenue per partner

    G5. Ideal Partner Profile

    Must-Have Criteria

    Criterion Requirement
    Market Access Existing customer base in target verticals (UAV, critical infra, robotics)
    Technical Capability Can integrate SDK (ROS/PX4 experience preferred)
    Support Capacity Can provide Tier 1 support to customers
    Sales Commitment Dedicated sales resource (at least part-time)
    Revenue Potential $200K+ annual revenue potential

    Nice-to-Have

    • Existing autonomy/AI practice
    • Compliance expertise (DO-178C, ISO 26262)
    • Multi-region presence
    • Strong brand in vertical

    G6. Partner Tiers & Benefits

    Silver Partner (Entry Level)

    Requirements: - Complete onboarding - 1+ certified technical resource - $50K+ annual revenue commitment

    Benefits: - 20% reseller margin - Standard support (48-hour response) - Access to partner portal - Co-branded collateral

    Gold Partner (Established)

    Requirements: - 6+ months as Silver - 3+ certified technical resources - $200K+ annual revenue - 2+ customer references

    Benefits: - 25% reseller margin - Priority support (24-hour response) - MDF (5% of annual sales) - Joint case studies - Early access to new features

    Platinum Partner (Strategic)

    Requirements: - 12+ months as Gold - 5+ certified technical resources - $500K+ annual revenue - Regional/vertical leadership

    Benefits: - 30% reseller margin - Dedicated partner manager - MDF (10% of annual sales) - Exclusive territory (optional) - Product roadmap input - Co-development opportunities

    G8. Partner Conflict Resolution

    Deal Registration Rules

    1. First to register wins (protected for 90 days)
    2. Customer choice: If customer has existing relationship with Partner B, Partner A's registration is void
    3. Overlap: If both partners have legitimate claim, split margin 50/50

    Territory Conflicts

    • Non-exclusive by default (except Platinum partners with negotiated exclusivity)
    • Vertical exclusivity: Possible for specialized partners (e.g., energy-only)

    G9. Partner Success Metrics

    Partner Health Score

    Metric Weight Target
    Revenue 40% $200K+/year
    Deal velocity 20% 3+ deals/year
    Customer satisfaction 20% NPS ≥ 50
    Certification 10% 3+ certified resources
    Engagement 10% Quarterly business reviews attended

    Action: Partners below 60% health score receive intervention (support, training, or off-boarding)

    G10. Co-Marketing Programs

    Joint Webinars (Quarterly)

    Format: - 45-minute technical deep dive - Partner presents use case - sbsQ presents technology - Live Q&A

    Promotion: - Both parties promote to email lists - Social media amplification - Recording published as lead gen asset

    Case Studies (2–4/year)

    Process: 1. Identify successful customer deployment 2. Interview customer + partner 3. Write case study (sbsQ drafts, partner reviews) 4. Publish on both websites 5. Promote via PR, social, email

    Incentive: $5K MDF credit per published case study

    Conference Co-Sponsorship

    Target Events: - AUVSI XPONENTIAL (UAV) - ASIS International (security) - Automate (robotics) - Regional defense/aerospace events

    Model: - sbsQ sponsors booth - Partner co-exhibits (shared space) - Joint presentations/demos

    G11. Partner Feedback Loop

    Quarterly Partner Advisory Board

    Participants: Top 5–10 partners (Gold/Platinum)

    Agenda: - Product roadmap review - Partner input on priorities - Market trends discussion - Process improvements

    Outcome: Partners feel heard, we get market intelligence

    Annual Partner Summit

    Format: 2-day event (virtual or in-person)

    Day 1: Business - Company vision + roadmap - Partner awards - Networking

    Day 2: Technical - Deep dives on new features - Hands-on labs - Certification exams

    G12. Partner Agreement (Key Terms)

    Standard Terms

    • Non-exclusive (unless negotiated)
    • 1-year initial term (auto-renew)
    • 30-day termination (either party, with cause)
    • Confidentiality (mutual NDA)
    • No minimum commitment (Silver tier)

    Gold/Platinum Terms

    • Minimum revenue commitment (Gold: $200K, Platinum: $500K)
    • Exclusive territory (Platinum only, negotiated)
    • Co-development rights (Platinum only)

    G13. Partner Compensation Models

    Model 1: Reseller Margin (Default)

    • Partner buys at discount, resells at list
    • Margin: 20–30% depending on tier
    • Partner owns pricing (within guidelines)

    Model 2: Revenue Share (Alternative)

    • sbsQ invoices customer directly
    • Partner receives % of annual revenue
    • Share: 15–25% depending on tier
    • Simpler for partners (no invoicing/collections)

    Model 3: Referral Fee (Passive)

    • Partner refers, sbsQ closes
    • Fee: 10% of first-year revenue (one-time)
    • For partners who can't/won't integrate
