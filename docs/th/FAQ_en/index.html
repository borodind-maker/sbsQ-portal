    BLOCK I: Hardware Breakthrough & Energy Efficiency

    How "simple" hardware outpaces giants

    2. On Ternary Logic: "Why is -1, 0, 1 better than standard 0 and 1?"

    Client: "The whole world runs on binary logic, and it's fine."

    Explanation: Binary logic (Yes/No) forces the computer to work constantly because 10/3=3.33333... This burns energy.

    Our ternary logic adds the "0" state (Superposition) — the "right to doubt." The drone does not make a decision until it is 100% certain. This stops "computational slavery" and allows instant operation once resonance is achieved.

    3. On Energy Efficiency: "Where is the proof that the battery will last longer?"

    Client: "Your software cannot change the physics of the battery."

    Explanation: The battery's physics — no, but the usage's physics — yes. A standard neural network constantly "heats the air" by performing billions of unnecessary multiplications.

    Our Multiplication-Free architecture simply doesn't do them. We reduced energy consumption per logical operation from 5.2 to 0.3 picojoules. It's like replacing an old incandescent bulb with a modern LED — same light, 17 times less power.

    4. On Hardware Agnosticism: "How does the code work on any equipment?"

    Client: "Quantum Bridge sounds like science fiction. How can code switch between a regular CPU and a quantum computer without rewriting?"

    Explanation: Imagine a bridge connecting two shores: one is the classical computing world, the other is the quantum world.

    Our Quantum Bridge is exactly that bridge. It emulates quantum logic on a standard processor via mathematical imitation (EMU-mode with golden ratio), and uses true parallelism on real quantum hardware (QPU). This makes SBSQ universal: from a smartphone to a supercomputer, without loss of efficiency.
