    Version: 1.0
    Last Updated: 2026-01-14
    Status: Complete

    📋 Overview

    This directory contains the complete business, market, and commercial strategy for SmartBees Quantum (sbsQ), the physics-gated autonomy platform.

    Purpose: Provide ready-to-use materials for: - Investor presentations - Customer pitches - Partner recruitment - Strategic planning - Sales enablement

    📚 Document Library

    1. Go-To-Market Strategy GTM_STRATEGY.md

    Audience: Leadership, investors, strategic planners

    Contents: - Problem statement (market pain points) - Solution overview (physics-gated grounding) - Core differentiators (measurable advantages) - Target markets (UAV, critical infrastructure, robotics, mesh networks) - Phased GTM approach (Proof → Scale → Ecosystem) - Competitive positioning - Success metrics

    Use When: Planning market entry, investor discussions, strategic reviews

    🇺🇦 Українська версія: GTM_STRATEGY_UA.md - гібридна версія з детальними ICP та KPI

    2. Executive One-Pager EXEC_ONE_PAGER.md

    Audience: C-level executives, decision makers

    Contents: - What we sell (1-sentence pitch) - Value proposition (fewer incidents, greater availability, audit control) - Target customers - Unique features (ACCEPT/UNKNOWN/REJECT policy) - KPI guarantees (pilot commitments) - Business model (licensing, support, services)

    Use When: Executive briefings, quick pitches, board presentations

    Format: Designed to fit on 1–2 pages when printed

    🇺🇦 Українська версія: EXEC_ONE_PAGER_UA.md - для локальних презентацій

    3. Investor Deck Outline INVESTOR_DECK_OUTLINE.md

    Audience: Investors, strategic partners

    Contents: - 10-slide presentation structure - Key points for each slide - Visual element suggestions - Speaker notes - Appendix slides (backup) - Opening/closing scripts

    Use When: Fundraising, partnership pitches, strategic investment discussions

    Format: Ready to convert to PowerPoint/Keynote

    4. Technical Dossier TECHNICAL_DOSSIER.md

    Audience: CTOs, principal engineers, technical evaluators

    Contents: - System architecture (modules, data flow) - Gate algorithms (z-score, χ², Mahalanobis) - Performance metrics (latency, resource requirements) - Integration patterns (ROS, PX4, standalone) - Validation & testing approach

    Use When: Technical due diligence, integration planning, architecture reviews

    Depth: 30-minute technical deep dive

    5. Pilot Kit PILOT_KIT.md

    Audience: Pilot customers, integration partners

    Contents: - Flagship demonstration scenario (wire landing under degradation) - Test matrix (7 scenarios minimum) - Execution plan (4-phase, 4–8 weeks) - Metrics collection (real-time + aggregate) - Deliverables (report, sensor profiles, replay package, Safety Case) - Pricing (fixed-fee packages) - Success criteria (KPI-based acceptance)

    Use When: Pilot proposals, POC planning, customer onboarding

    Format: Complete pilot program guide

    6. Pricing Packages PRICING_PACKAGES.md

    Audience: Sales, customers, partners

    Contents: - SDK licensing tiers (Base, Pro, Enterprise, Defense) - Pilot program pricing - Professional services (integration, training, support) - Add-on modules (à la carte) - Volume discounts - Payment terms - ROI calculator

    Use When: Quote generation, contract negotiations, pricing discussions

    Format: Comprehensive pricing reference

    7. Partnership Strategy PARTNERSHIPS.md

    Audience: Potential partners, business development

    Contents: - Partner categories (Integration, Technology, Channel) - Enablement package (technical, sales, marketing, commercial) - Onboarding process (4-phase) - Partner tiers (Silver, Gold, Platinum) - Compensation models (reseller, rev-share, referral) - Co-marketing programs - Partner agreement terms

    Use When: Partner recruitment, ecosystem development, channel strategy

    Format: Complete partnership playbook

    8. Product Roadmap ROADMAP.md

    Audience: Internal teams, investors, strategic partners

    Contents: - 36-month development plan - Phase 1 (0–6m): Proof & Validation - Phase 2 (6–18m): Scale & Productization - Phase 3 (18–36m): Ecosystem & Standards - Engineering deliverables (by quarter) - Business milestones - Investment requirements - Revenue projections

    Use When: Strategic planning, investor updates, resource allocation

    Format: Quarterly breakdown with success criteria

    9. FAQ FAQ.md

    Audience: Sales teams, customers, partners

    Contents: - Technical questions (20 Q&A) - Business questions - Competitive questions - Pricing & commercial questions - Implementation questions - Roadmap & future questions

    Use When: Sales enablement, objection handling, customer education

    Format: Ready-to-use answers for common questions
