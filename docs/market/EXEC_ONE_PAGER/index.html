    SmartBees Quantum (sbsQ) - Physics-Gated Autonomy Platform

    Document Type: Executive Summary
    Version: 1.0
    Last Updated: 2026-01-14
    Classification: Business Confidential

    What We Sell

    A module/SDK that forces AI to be "grounded" in physics: every LLM/VLG hypothesis must pass physics-statistical verification before being accepted.

    The Problem

    Autonomous systems fail in the real world because: 1. Sensors conflict (IMU vs GNSS vs vision give incompatible data) 2. Communications degrade (jamming, spoofing, link loss) 3. AI hallucinates (LLMs generate plausible-sounding facts without proof)

    Result: Mission failures, safety incidents, compliance barriers, procurement resistance.

    Our Solution

    Physics-Gated Grounding: Every AI claim passes through a statistical verification gate: - z-score for 1D residuals - χ² (chi-squared) for multi-dimensional Mahalanobis distance - Decision policy: ACCEPT / UNKNOWN / REJECT

    If the hypothesis contradicts physics → REJECT → safe mode
    If data is insufficient → UNKNOWN → re-measure, alternate sensor, peer check
    If verified → ACCEPT → proceed with mission

    Value Proposition

    Benefit Why It Matters
    Fewer incidents AI doesn't "invent facts" in critical control loops
    Greater availability Controlled degradation (UNKNOWN → recover, not crash)
    Audit & control Decisions are explainable via residual metrics + covariance validation
