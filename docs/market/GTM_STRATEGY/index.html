    SmartBees Quantum (sbsQ) - Physics-Gated Autonomy Platform

    Document Type: Strategic Planning
    Version: 1.0
    Last Updated: 2026-01-14
    Classification: Business Confidential

    A1. Problem Statement (Market Pain)

    Autonomous systems (UAV/UGV/AGV/perimeter robots/mesh networks) degrade in real-world environments due to three critical failure classes:

    1. Communication & Navigation Degradation

    • GNSS jamming/spoofing
    • Signal shadowing and multipath interference
    • Link disruption in contested environments

    2. Sensor Modality Conflicts

    • IMU, GNSS, vision, magnetometer, barometer provide incompatible estimates
    • No unified ground truth when sensors disagree
    • Traditional fusion fails under adversarial conditions

    3. Reasoning Model Uncertainty

    • LLM/VLG can generate hypotheses without sufficient evidence
    • "Hallucinated facts" in critical control loops
    • Black-box AI creates operational risk and compliance barriers

    Impact: Mission failures, incident cost escalation, compliance complexity, procurement barriers.

    A2. Solution Overview

    SmartBees Quantum (sbsQ) is an autonomy platform where every LLM/VLG statement is accepted only after deterministic physics-statistical verification in the space of measurable quantities (position/velocity/angle/size/time/energy).

    Core Approach: Physics-Gated Grounding

    The model can propose a hypothesis, but cannot "make it true" without passing gate verification: - z-score (1D residuals) - χ² (multi-dimensional Mahalanobis distance) - Statistical thresholds calibrated to operational modes

    Decision Policy: ACCEPT / UNKNOWN / REJECT

    A3. Core Differentiators (Measurable)

    Feature Benefit Measurable Outcome
    Physics Gate as Safety Policy Layer Unifies truthfulness control across all AI outputs FP/FN rates on spoof detection
    Ternary Logic (−1/0/+1) Controlled degradation: UNKNOWN is a safe operational state % missions without operator intervention
    Entropy Starvation Policy LLM learns to "ask, don't guess" under UNKNOWN/REJECT Reduction in unsafe actions
    Verification & Replay Generates audit artifacts (residual logs, thresholds, decisions) Safety Case compliance artifacts

    A4. Target Markets

    Primary Markets

    1. UAV Operations / Fleet Security

    • Pain: Autonomy degrades under sensor/comms degradation
    • Value: Controlled degradation, mission continuity
    • Size: Global UAV fleet management market ($4.2B by 2028)

    2. Critical Infrastructure

    • Sectors: Energy, perimeter security, pipeline monitoring
    • Pain: Audit requirements, fail-safe mandates, error accountability
    • Value: Explainable AI decisions, compliance-ready artifacts
    • Size: Critical infrastructure protection market ($148B by 2027)

    3. Robotics/Logistics (AGV/Indoor Autonomy)

    • Pain: Instability in complex environments (obstacles, poor lighting, reflections)
    • Value: Robust navigation, reduced incident rates
    • Size: AGV market ($7.8B by 2030)

    4. Private Secure Networks / Mesh

    • Pain: Outlier tolerance, data integrity in distributed systems
    • Value: Policy-verified decisions and messages
    • Size: Private 5G/mesh networks ($8.3B by 2029)

    A5. Go-to-Market (Phased Approach)

    Phase 1: Proof + Pilot Kit (0–6 months)

    Objective: Establish credibility through measurable proof

    Deliverables: - Demonstration scenario "before/after" with baseline comparison - Metrics: FP/FN rates, recovery time, UNKNOWN-rate distribution - 2–3 pilot deployments with integration partners

    Success Criteria: - Zero unsafe actions in controlled scenarios - Detection TPR > 95% on injected anomalies - Partner commitment to Phase 2

    Phase 2: SDK Licensing (6–18 months)

    Objective: Scale through productization

    Deliverables: - SDK packages: Base / Pro / Enterprise - Integration adapters for common stacks (ROS, PX4, ArduPilot) - Calibration toolkit v1 (offline, batch processing) - Safety Case template library

    Revenue Model: - Per-device licensing - Per-fleet/per-site licensing - Support/SLA packages

    Success Criteria: - 10+ paying customers across 2+ verticals - Revenue: $500K–$1M ARR - Customer retention > 80%

    Phase 3: Ecosystem Development (18–36 months)

    Objective: Build developer ecosystem and market leadership

    Deliverables: - DevKit with simulator, replay tools, sensor profile library - Marketplace for modules/plugins - Partner network of certified integrators - Standards contribution (audit log formats, replay protocols)

    Revenue Model: - Platform revenue share - Professional services - Enterprise SLA + custom integration

    Success Criteria: - 50+ active integrations - 3rd-party developer community (100+ developers) - Revenue: $3M–$5M ARR - Market recognition as physics-grounded AI safety standard
