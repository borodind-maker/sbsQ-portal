    SmartBees Quantum (sbsQ) - 10-Slide Presentation Structure

    Document Type: Investor Relations
    Version: 1.0
    Last Updated: 2026-01-14
    Classification: Business Confidential

    Deck Overview

    Purpose: Secure strategic investment and pilot partnerships
    Duration: 15–20 minutes + Q&A
    Audience: Investors, strategic partners, potential pilot customers

    📊 Slide 1: The Thesis

    Title

    "Autonomy Breaks in the Real World"

    Key Points

    • Autonomous systems degrade due to:
    • Sensor conflicts (IMU ≠ GNSS ≠ vision)
    • Communication failures (jam/spoof/link loss)
    • AI hallucinations ("plausible facts" without proof)

    Visual Elements

    • Split-screen comparison: Lab vs Real World
    • Icons: drone with warning symbols, conflicting sensor readings
    • Stat callout: "X% of autonomous missions fail due to sensor/comms degradation"

    Speaker Notes

    "The gap between lab performance and field reliability is the trillion-dollar autonomy problem. Systems that work perfectly in simulation fail catastrophically when sensors disagree or AI guesses wrong."

    📊 Slide 2: The Solution

    Title

    "Physics-Gated Grounding: Make AI Prove It"

    Key Points

    • ACCEPT / UNKNOWN / REJECT policy layer
    • Every LLM/VLG hypothesis verified against physics
    • z-score / χ² / Mahalanobis distance gates
    • Entropy Starvation: LLM learns to ask, not guess

    Visual Elements

    • Flow diagram: Hypothesis → Physics Gate → Decision
    • Three-zone visualization (ACCEPT/UNKNOWN/REJECT)
    • Code snippet (simplified gate logic)

    Speaker Notes

    "sbsQ is a policy layer, not another AI model. It forces AI to prove claims with measurable physics before acting. If the residual is too large, the system doesn't hallucinate — it asks for better data."

    📊 Slide 3: Why Now

    Title

    "Autonomy Scaling Outpaces Certification"

    Key Points

    1. Regulatory pressure: AI explainability mandates emerging
    2. Deployment reality: Real-world failures expose sensor brittleness
    3. Procurement barriers: "Black-box AI" blocks critical infrastructure sales
    4. Safety incidents: High-profile failures increase liability concerns

    Visual Elements

    • Timeline: Autonomy adoption curve vs certification capability gap
    • News headlines (anonymized) of autonomous system failures
    • Market size projections for safety-critical autonomy

    Speaker Notes

    "The market is moving faster than trust. Our customers need audit-ready, fail-safe autonomy yesterday. Traditional approaches can't bridge this gap."

    📊 Slide 4: How It Works (Technical Overview)

    Title

    "Physics Verification in 3 Steps"

    Key Points

    Step 1: Hypothesis Parsing

    • LLM/VLG makes claim: "Target is 12m ahead at 3° bearing"
    • System extracts measurable parameters (distance, angle)

    Step 2: Physics Gate

    • Compare to sensor estimates (EKF/SLAM/tracker)
    • Calculate residual + covariance
    • Compute z-score (1D) or χ² (multi-D)

    Step 3: Policy Decision

    • z < threshold → ACCEPT → proceed
    • z > threshold → REJECT → safe mode
    • Insufficient data → UNKNOWN → re-measure

    Visual Elements

    • 3-panel illustration with icons
    • Sample calculation (z-score example)
    • Decision tree visualization

    Speaker Notes

    "This isn't magic. It's classical statistics applied as a safety constraint on AI. The innovation is making it real-time, adaptive, and auditable."

    📊 Slide 5: Demo (Before/After)

    Title

    "Measurable Improvement: Pilot Results"

    Key Points

    • Scenario: Wire landing under GNSS spoofing + magnetometer jamming
    • Baseline system:
    • 15% unsafe actions
    • 45% mission failures
    • No audit trail
    • With sbsQ:
    • 0% unsafe actions
    • 95% controlled degradation (UNKNOWN → recovery)
    • Full verification logs

    Visual Elements

    • Side-by-side metrics comparison (table or bar chart)
    • Video thumbnail: demo scenario
    • Callout: "Zero unsafe actions in 1,000 test cycles"

    Speaker Notes

    "This is one example from our validation suite. In every scenario, sbsQ eliminates unsafe actions while maintaining mission success through controlled degradation."
