    🗝️ Logic Block Legend

    1. smartbees/core: The Foundation. Essential classes, thread management, and event loops.
    2. smartbees/brain: The Intelligence. Quantum logic, probabilistic decision-making, and self-reflection.
    3. smartbees/app: The Application. Mission logic, autopilot, and real-time execution scenarios.
    4. smartbees/sensors: The Perception. Hardware interfaces for environmental awareness.
    5. smartbees/comm: The Connectivity. Protocols for internal swarm synchronization and external interfaces.

    Note: This structure follows a Domain-Driven Design (DDD) approach to ensure maximum modularity and resilience.
