    This section contains descriptions of simulations conducted to verify the architecture stability and mathematical models of the SmartBees Quantum project.

    🔬 Detailed Overview (Cases)

    1. "Triple-Core Council" Simulation (Agent Council)

    Goal: Verify the stability of TCI decision-making logic in the presence of conflicting sensor data. - Scenario: 3 agents receive data with +20% noise. - Conclusion: The system transitions to an "Uncertain" (Neutro) state instead of making a false-positive decision. This is critical for safety.

    2. "GHS Precision" Simulation

    Goal: Verify cumulative error when using the Global Heartbeat Sync (GHS) protocol for time tick synchronization. - Result: Time drift was less than 0.5 ms over 24 hours of simulation.

    3. "GHS-Harmonic Threshold Swarm" Simulation

    Goal: Verify energy efficiency of sensor distribution at a 137.5° angle.

    4. "Electronic Storm" Simulation (EW Resilience)

    Goal: Verify the system's ability to navigate and complete a mission under total GPS suppression and communication channel jamming. - Result: The drone switched to TCI filtering (Neutro state) and GHS synchronization. Control loop frequency was 50.68 Hz. Detected 100% of interference attempts. - Details: See the dedicated report Offensive EW Resilience.
